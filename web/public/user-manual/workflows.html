Workflow — Analyze a New Symbol

End-to-end walkthrough from zero to a fully-analyzed position.

Sync underlying OHLCV bars

Data & Symbols → enter the symbol and date range → click Sync. Or use the CLI: scripts/sync_data.py --source massive --symbol AAPL --from 2024-01-01 --to 2024-12-31

Check the Volatility Dashboard

Volatility → select symbol → Analyze. Note the IV Rank and the Strategy Guidance recommendation before touching the Research page.

Run a forecast

Research & AI → Run Forecast. Choose ARIMA for speed or GB for a non-linear read. Horizon 1–5 is most useful for near-term option selection.

Evaluate the right strategy for the vol environment

The Volatility Dashboard said high IVR → try an iron condor or credit spread. Enter strikes around the expected move boundaries and click Evaluate strategy.

Enter the premium and check break-even

Fill in "Premium paid" to see the break-even prices on the results card. Confirm the range is comfortable relative to the Expected Move from the Volatility Dashboard.

Size the position

Position Sizing card → enter capital, win rate, avg win/loss → Calculate. Use the Half-Kelly % or Fixed Risk output to determine how much capital to commit.

Workflow — Let the Tool Pick a Strategy

Use automated strategy discovery when you are unsure which structure fits best.

POST /strategy-engine/discover
{
  "symbol": "AAPL",
  "from_date": "2024-01-01",
  "to_date": "2024-06-30",
  "horizon": 5,
  "model": "arima"
}

The engine will:

  1. Run a forecast on the symbol/range.
  2. Evaluate all six strategy types with auto-generated parameters (±2% width by default).
  3. Return them ranked by expected value descending.

Use the top result as a starting point, then fine-tune strike widths and verify break-even prices manually in the Evaluate strategy card.

Workflow — Size a Position

Never enter a trade without knowing your position size in advance. Here is the recommended decision tree:

Know your edge estimate.

From past backtests or forward-test data: what is your historical win rate on this strategy type? What is the average win vs. average loss? Be honest — edge estimates degrade in live trading.

Enter your numbers in the Position Sizing card.

Conservative defaults: 55% win rate, avg win 1.5× avg loss.

Read Half-Kelly % — this is your risk budget for this trade.

Example: Half-Kelly = 5% of $10,000 = $500 at risk.

Compute contracts from the risk budget.

If the strategy's max loss per contract is $200 (net debit × 100 multiplier), then $500 / $200 = 2 contracts max.

Cross-check with the Fixed Risk % rule.

Many traders cap individual trade risk at 1–2% of capital regardless of Kelly. Use whichever is more conservative.
